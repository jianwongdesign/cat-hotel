# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Cat owners arranging boarding in Singapore. They need to see the actual rooms, compare capacity and price, choose dates and guests, and understand the booking and payment journey.

## Product Purpose

Little Pawcious cat-hotel website design preview. The user requested room showcasing, booking and payment as the core eventual functions, with a Git-backed shared preview.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The current working prototype includes room galleries, guest capacity, dates, estimates, guest details and payment-method selection. Reservations and payment collection are not connected. Preserve that visible preview boundary. Actual unit inventory, merchant credentials and operating policies remain production decisions; never invent availability or successful payment.

## Brand Commitments

Use the existing Little Pawcious website https://littlepawcious.sg/ as the factual and brand reference. User confirmed a cosy boutique-hotel feel, then rejected the implementation as too plain and empty and requested a full redesign with more presence. Build the working page directly. Desktop mouse cursor should be a paw; mobile must be ready.

## Evidence on Hand

Actual room and cat photographs in public/images; source mapping in docs/DESIGN-HANDOFF.md. Existing Higgsfield cat-walking film in public/video, labelled AI-created atmosphere footage. It cost 19.5 of the authorised 35-credit video cap. This redesign reuses it without further generation. No testimonials or review scores have been supplied.

## Product Principles

- Let actual room imagery and clear capacity/rates support comparison.
- Keep the booking action easy to find on desktop and touch devices.
- Distinguish the design preview from real inventory and payment processing.
- Preserve the cosy cat-focused identity without generic ornament or fabricated proof.
